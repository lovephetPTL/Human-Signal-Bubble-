# Human-Signal-Bubble-
A living being does not “disappear” at the moment of cessation. It separates into the constituent elements of spirit and returns to the Field, rejoining the current of cyclical becoming—what we call rebirth.


At the moment of extinction, what remains is the body and the spirit.
The body dissolves.
The spirit disperses into the Field.
In that same interval, the spirits of all beings—small and large—also converge into this Field.
It is the same interval in which conception occurs: a spirit moves out from the Field, directs itself toward a new body, and fuses with the first dividing cell, gradually forming another life.
There is no gap between these two movements.
No pause. No waiting state.
The Field therefore becomes a point of continuous exchange—
a place where accumulated imprints enter, and from which a new configuration emerges.
The spirit that arrives is not empty.
It carries traces: the instinct to hunt, the memory of loneliness, fear, stillness, and social impulse—
experiences belonging to both humans and animals.
When these imprints combine with a forming body, they produce a new signature of a person.


## Concept

This project explores a hypothesis called **Human Signal Bubble** —
a model describing consciousness as a transient signal pattern (ψ)
that dissolves and recomposes within a non-temporal field.
