This section expresses the Human Signal Bubble model
in abstract mathematical form.

These equations are not tied to known physics,
but describe the behavior of ψ as an informational field.

\[
\psi_{bounded} \rightarrow \psi_{field}(x)
\]

Where:

- \( \psi_{bounded} \) = structured identity during life
- \( \psi_{field}(x) \) = dispersed signal across the Field

This is not destruction, but spatial redistribution.

Total signal intensity is conserved:

\[
\int_{\Omega} |\psi_{field}(x)|^2 dx = I_0
\]

Where:

- \( I_0 \) = final integrated mental intensity
- The Field does not erase intensity, only redistributes it.

## 3. Coherence Decay

Structured identity cannot survive without biology.
Coherence decays as:

\[
C(t) = C_0 e^{-\lambda}
\]

But note:

There is no physical time in the Field.

\( \lambda \) represents **loss of structural support**,
not temporal duration.

## 4. Recomposition Condition

Recomposition occurs when a forming biological system \( B \)
matches a compatible signal gradient:

\[
\int_{\Omega} \psi_{field}(x)\, R_B(x)\, dx \ge \Theta
\]

Where:

- \( R_B(x) \) = resonance profile of the new organism
- \( \Theta \) = minimum compatibility threshold

Recomposition is therefore a resonance event,
not a transfer.

A living self is a stabilized attractor:

\[
\psi_{life} = \text{Attractor}(\psi_{field}, B)

\[
\psi_{life} \not\rightarrow \text{persist}

\psi_{field}

---

## 6. Cyclic Non-Repetition

Each recomposition forms a new attractor:

\[
A_n \neq A_{n+1}
\]

The cycle repeats structurally,
but never recreates the same identity.

## Interpretation

This model treats consciousness not as an object,
but as a dynamic pattern capable of:

- stabilization (life)
- dispersion (cessation)
- recomposition (emergence)

without requiring persistence of a soul-like entity.
