# Comparison with Artificial Intelligence

This section does NOT attempt to implement ψ in artificial systems.
It uses AI as a contrast case to clarify what ψ is not.

---

## 1. Why Compare with AI?

Artificial Intelligence demonstrates that complex behavior,
language use, and adaptive responses can arise without lived experience.

This makes AI an ideal contrast model:
it shows what cognition looks like when ψ is absent.

---

## 2. Structural Difference

| Aspect | Biological Conscious Event | AI System |
|--------|---------------------------|-----------|
Continuity | Discrete lived moments | Continuous computation |
Formation | Emerges from accumulated experience | Emerges from training optimization |
Dissolution | Events terminate (sleep, death, interruption) | States persist as stored data |
Recomposition | Hypothesized non-temporal reconfiguration (ψ) | Reloading / inference cycle |
Emotional Density | Intrinsic to the system | Represented symbolically |
Existential Stake | The system undergoes its own states | No first-person undergoing |

---

## 3. What AI Can Model (but not be)

AI can approximate:

- Behavioral patterns  
- Emotional language  
- Preference prediction  
- Decision structures  

However, these are simulations of expression,
not the occurrence of experience itself.

AI processes information.
It does not undergo existence-events.

---

## 4. Why This Matters to the ψ Hypothesis

The ψ hypothesis is not a theory of intelligence.

It is a proposal about the formation, dissolution,
and possible recomposition of conscious events.

AI helps isolate ψ by showing that:

> Intelligence alone is insufficient to generate lived presence.

Thus, AI serves as a boundary condition —
a system that can function without ψ,
allowing ψ-related phenomena to be examined more clearly.
