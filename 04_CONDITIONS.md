# Conditions for Recomposition

Recomposition of ψ does not occur randomly.

It follows specific compatibility conditions between
dissolved signal intensities and emerging biological systems.

## 1. Intensity Residue

When ψ decomposes, it does not vanish into zero.
It disperses as gradients of intensity.

Higher coherence during biological life produces
stronger structured residues.

These are not memories —
they are stability tendencies.

## 2. Biological Resonance Window

A newly forming organism creates a resonance window:

- neural complexity threshold
- metabolic continuity
- signal integration capacity

When this window aligns with compatible ψ gradients,
recomposition naturally occurs.

No selection.
No assignment.
Only compatibility.

## 3. Temporary Identity Stabilization

Identity exists only while a biological structure
can maintain coherence.

Selfhood is therefore:

a stabilization event, not an entity.

When coherence collapses, ψ re-enters dispersion.

## 4. No Persistence Without Structure

There is no “holding state” between lives.

Without a biological substrate:

ψ cannot remain as a person,
only as distributed signal tendency.

## 5. Cyclic but Non-Repetitive

Recomposition is cyclic in process,
but never repeats an identity.

Each stabilization is a new configuration,
built from redistributed intensities.

The cycle is structural —
not personal.
